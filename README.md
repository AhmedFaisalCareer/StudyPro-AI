# 🤖 StudyPro AI – AI-Powered Study Helper

StudyPro AI is an AI-powered study assistant built with **Python, Streamlit, Google Gemini AI, and Firebase**.

It helps students study smarter by generating notes, quizzes, flashcards, explanations, mind maps, practice questions, and personalized study plans.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- View your study statistics
- Track XP and level
- View study streak
- Quick access to study tools

### 🤖 AI Study Assistant
Ask questions about any subject or topic and get AI-powered responses.

### 📚 Smart Notes
Generate organized study notes for any topic.

Features include:
- Detailed notes
- Key points
- Important definitions
- Easy-to-understand explanations

### 📝 Quiz Generator
Generate AI-powered quizzes based on your selected subject and topic.

- Multiple-choice questions
- Automatic scoring
- Score tracking
- XP rewards

### 🧠 Flashcards
Create AI-generated flashcards to help remember important concepts.

### 💡 Concept Explainer
Get difficult concepts explained in simple language.

Useful for:
- Science
- Mathematics
- Computer Science
- Geography
- History
- And other subjects

### 🗺️ Mind Maps
Generate structured mind maps to understand relationships between ideas and topics.

### 📅 Study Planner
Create personalized study plans based on your subjects and study goals.

### 📈 Progress Tracking
Track your learning progress through:
- XP
- Levels
- Quiz scores
- Study statistics
- Streaks

### 🔥 Firebase Integration
Firebase Realtime Database is used to store:
- Student profiles
- Study history
- Quiz statistics
- Progress data
